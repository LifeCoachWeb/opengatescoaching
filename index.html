You can begin here

Everyone start somewhere.

Most people don’t reach out because everything is clear. They reach out because a decision keeps circling, a habit is hard to change, or life no longer feels quite right.

Your first conversation is a place to slow down and say what has been weighing on you. Harold or Roxanne will listen, ask questions, and help you decide what would be useful to work on.

Appointments are available virtually and in person. In-person meeting details are shared after your appointment is confirmed.

Two coaching options

Start with the person and conversation that fit your needs.

You may know exactly what you want to work on. You may only know that you’re tired of feeling stuck. Either is enough for a first conversation.

A clear note about coaching

Coaching can be helpful, but it is not psychotherapy, medical care, psychiatric care, nutrition treatment, or emergency help. Harold and Roxanne do not diagnose or treat medical or mental health conditions.

If you are living with a serious mental health condition, continue working with a qualified healthcare professional. For immediate or emergency help, call 911 or the appropriate emergency service.
