You can begin here

Everyone starts somewhere.

Most people do not reach out because everything is clear. They reach out because a decision keeps circling, a pattern is hard to change, or life no longer feels quite right.

Your first conversation is a place to slow down and say what has been weighing on you. Harold or Roxanne will listen, ask thoughtful questions, and help you decide what would be useful to work on.

Appointments are available virtually and in person. In-person meeting details are shared after your appointment is confirmed.

Life coach

Harold Gates

This is important.

Coaching can be helpful, but it is not psychotherapy, medical care, psychiatric care, nutrition treatment, or emergency help. Harold and Roxanne do not diagnose or treat medical or mental health conditions.

If you are living with a serious mental health or medical condition, continue working with a qualified healthcare professional. For immediate or emergency help, call 911 or the appropriate emergency service.
