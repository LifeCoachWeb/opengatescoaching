How coaching works

A useful conversation should lead somewhere.

Harold and Roxanne will listen, ask direct questions, and help you turn the conversation into a next step you can use.

01

Start with what is happening.

Tell your coach what feels hard, unclear, or stuck. You do not need a polished story.

02

03

Leave with something clear and realistic to try before the next session.

Meet the coaches

Two coaches. Two areas of support.

You can ask for Harold or Roxanne when you book. Not sure who to choose? Tell us what you want help with, and we will point you in the right direction.

01

Life coach

Harold Gates

Harold helps people who feel stuck, pulled in too many directions, or unsure what to do next.

His style is practical and direct. He asks clear questions, helps you sort out the problem, and keeps the focus on choices you can use in daily life.

Major decisions Life changes Goals and follow-through Relationships Direction and motivation

Roxanne Gates

Roxanne offers nonclinical support for people working on eating disorder recovery, body image, routines, and setbacks.

Accessibility

We want this website to work for you.

Open Gates Counseling & Coaching is working to make this site easy to use for people with disabilities. We use WCAG 2.1 Level AA as our working guide. That includes clear headings, keyboard access, visible focus, readable color contrast, text descriptions for meaningful images, and support for reduced motion.

Accessibility is ongoing. The scheduling calendar and client forms open on Google websites, so Google controls those tools.

If something on this site is hard to use, call 217-371-0506 or email coachopengates@gmail.com. Tell us which page or task caused trouble and what format would work better. We will do our best to help.

The U.S. Department of Justice uses WCAG 2.1 Level AA in its 2024 Title II web rule for state and local governments. Open Gates is a private coaching practice, but we use the same standard as a useful benchmark. Read the Department of Justice fact sheet Opens in a new tab
