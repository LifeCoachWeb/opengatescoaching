How coaching can help

You may not need a big plan. You may need a good next step.

Coaching gives you time to think out loud, look at the problem from another angle, and decide what makes sense for your life right now.

01

A hard decision

Talk through the options, name what matters most, and choose a practical way forward.

02

A change in your life

Find your footing after a new role, a move, a loss, or a change in a relationship.

03

A goal that keeps stalling

Break the goal into smaller steps and build follow-through that fits your real schedule.

04

Stress that is hard to sort out

Slow things down, notice the pattern, and choose one manageable change at a time.

Common questions

What people usually ask.

It is normal to have questions before you book. Here are the basics.

Still have a question?
coachopengates@gmail.com

Is coaching the same as therapy or counseling?

No. Open Gates provides coaching only. Coaching can help with goals, routines, accountability, decisions, and personal action. It does not diagnose or treat medical or mental health conditions.

What happens during the complimentary consultation?

You can explain what led you to reach out, ask questions, and get a feel for the coach's style. Then you decide whether you want to schedule another session.

Do I need to choose Harold or Roxanne before I book?

No. The calendar is shared. You can request a coach or say that you are not sure. A short note about what you want help with is enough.

Can Mental Health Coaching replace therapy or medical care?

No. Mental Health Coaching is nonclinical support. It does not replace therapy, psychiatric care, medical care, or emergency help. Keep working with qualified healthcare professionals when needed.

When should I complete the intake and agreement?

Complete them after the first call, once you and your coach decide to move forward. Do not use the forms for medical records or urgent concerns.
