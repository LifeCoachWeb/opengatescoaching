What happens in coaching

Good coaching is not advice dressed up as a conversation.

You bring the lived experience. Your coach listens, asks clear questions, helps you look at the situation from another angle, and works with you to turn insight into one useful action.

01

Name what is actually happening.

You do not need a polished explanation. Start with what feels confusing, heavy, frustrating, or unfinished.

02

Look at the pattern from another angle.

Thoughtful questions can uncover assumptions, choices, and possibilities that are hard to see when you are inside the problem.

03

Choose a next step that belongs to you.

Leave with an action that fits your values, your capacity, and the life you will return to after the session.
